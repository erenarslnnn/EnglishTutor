Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Elizaveta Tarasova ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit the trial-lesson evaluation form, we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, phone number, and any notes about your goals or scheduling preferences. We do not collect payment information through this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your contact details solely to respond to your inquiry, schedule a trial lesson, and share relevant information about lessons and tuition. We never sell or rent your information to third parties.

Cookies & Local Storage

This site does not use tracking cookies. Your language preference (English, Turkish, or Russian) is saved locally in your browser using localStorage, so the site remembers your choice on your next visit. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp or Telegram, your conversation is subject to that platform's own privacy policy. We are not responsible for how these third-party services handle your data.

Data Retention

We retain your contact information only for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and manage our tutoring relationship, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Your Rights & Contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us at hello@elizavetatarasova.com. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
